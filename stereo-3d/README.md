Stereo 3D samples: photos and films that open as ordinary pictures and films on any system, and show depth where the system reads the 3D mark.
Each file carries its format's own mark: MPO (CIPA DC-007) and JPS (APP3 descriptor) photos, a PNG with the sTER chunk (.pns), an MP4 with the H.264 frame packing SEI and st3d, and Matroska and WebM films with StereoMode.
Photos and films are full colour, left eye first, side by side (the MPO holds two JPEGs); none is red-cyan.
